# ADR-001: Transform sources and deliver candidates without stealing destination admission

**Repository:** Quantum-L9/l9-ingest  
**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Status in pack:** LOCKED_OPERATOR_DECISION  
**Implementation/deployment:** not performed by this delivery.

## Context
Coding-host ingestion became constellation infrastructure in the wrong repository.

## Decision
Ingest owns source-processing workflow, transform evidence and per-destination delivery. Destination nodes admit. Already-typed writes are not forced through ingestion.

## Alternatives not selected
One mandatory ingress tollbooth; direct destination database writes; promotion by producer assertion.

## Ownership and invariants
The semantic owner is this repository only for the responsibility named above. Imported platform invariants retain their original owners. Read BOUNDARY.yaml and INVARIANTS.md before implementing. No adapter, registry index or profile may silently redefine this decision.

## Consequences
Capture, parse, classify, extract and projection intents are distinct stages with bounded failure receipts.

## Required evidence
One destination admits while another times out; no duplicate or false aggregate success. Unit-level negative tests, installed-artifact parity and staging proofs are required where applicable; pack checks cannot satisfy them.

## Migration and rollback
Suspend unsettled deliveries and reconcile original keys; do not replay completed effects.

## Provenance
Cursor generated-data donor and locked two-lane design. This record preserves locked direction while detailed schema/implementation proposals remain subject to owner contract acceptance. It is not a retrospective assertion that code has been merged or deployed.
