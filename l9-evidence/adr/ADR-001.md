# ADR-001: Evidence and retrieval views do not replace source authority

**Repository:** Quantum-L9/l9-evidence  
**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Status in pack:** LOCKED_OPERATOR_DECISION  
**Implementation/deployment:** not performed by this delivery.

## Context
Documents, receipts and raw observations must be retained and queried without masquerading as business truth.

## Decision
Evidence owns exact source references, authorized retained copies and disposable retrieval views. Current business/approval truth remains at its source owner.

## Alternatives not selected
Mongo as all-domain truth; search score as authority; missing source revision replaced by best semantic match.

## Ownership and invariants
The semantic owner is this repository only for the responsibility named above. Imported platform invariants retain their original owners. Read BOUNDARY.yaml and INVARIANTS.md before implementing. No adapter, registry index or profile may silently redefine this decision.

## Consequences
Every derived item retains source coordinate and trust/use classification.

## Required evidence
Wrong hash, wrong scope, missing revision, revoked source and stale policy tests. Unit-level negative tests, installed-artifact parity and staging proofs are required where applicable; pack checks cannot satisfy them.

## Migration and rollback
Retire invalid views; retain lawful provenance and never claim upstream deletion from local erasure.

## Provenance
User Evidence/Memory/State distinction and uploaded research. This record preserves locked direction while detailed schema/implementation proposals remain subject to owner contract acceptance. It is not a retrospective assertion that code has been merged or deployed.
