# Game Theory and Strategic Interaction Origin

This v2 evolution was driven by the observation that many real decisions are not made against a passive world. Other decision-makers respond to our actions, update beliefs about us, generate signals strategically, and change the future state.

Key extracted insights:

- the state of the world includes time;
- every material action/observation can require decision-surface recalculation;
- poker is a clean toy domain for incomplete information, hidden state, signals, higher-order beliefs and action/reaction lookahead;
- business negotiation is often positive-sum or mixed-motive rather than zero-sum;
- strategic consistency/reputation may be more valuable in repeated business relationships than deliberate unpredictability;
- strategic influence can be truthful/cooperative and need not be deception;
- game theory sits across Probabilistic and Numerical Reasoning and uses Formal constraints, so Strategic Interaction is a composition rather than a new sibling;
- bounded lookahead, belief depth and Value of Computation prevent runaway recursion;
- no ML dependency is required for the first implementation.
