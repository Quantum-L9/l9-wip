# Meta-Decision and Strategic Leverage Origin

This v2.0 layer consolidates the architecture conversation that generalized the Reasoning Plane from domain decision evaluation into a reusable decision substrate for business and cognitive actions.

Locked user decisions include:

- information has value only when it can change a decision enough to improve expected utility;
- information acquisition must route to the best source, not always research;
- Value of Computation should provide a principled stop condition;
- human attention is a scarce resource and should be requested when consequences are material;
- business actions and cognitive actions may be evaluated through the same reasoning substrate;
- leverage must be measurable rather than a vague bonus;
- work made unnecessary is a preferred leverage outcome;
- resource-allocation answers may be portfolios such as 40/35/15/10 rather than a single winner;
- the broad question is which admissible next action has the highest Net Strategic Value.

Relevant donor boundaries:

- L9 Reasoning Triad First-Order/Second-Order/Leverage kernels remain external semantic owners for causal intervention selection, consequence modeling, and systemic leverage evaluation.
- The Research Node acquires external evidence under a declared strategy and does not become truth or reasoning authority.
