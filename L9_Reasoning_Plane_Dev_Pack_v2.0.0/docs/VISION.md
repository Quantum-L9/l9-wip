# Vision

## North star

Build a provider-neutral L9 reasoning substrate that lets agents and domain systems determine what behavior is rational in the current state instead of hard-coding large procedural decision trees.

## Three semantic claims

- Formal: **admissibility**
- Probabilistic: **belief**
- Numerical: **utility**

## Dynamic intelligence

Real decisions unfold through time. Signals change beliefs; actions change other actors' incentives and beliefs; their responses change outcomes; outcomes become new evidence. The plane therefore supports bounded state-transition reasoning and strategic interaction without turning itself into an autonomous orchestrator.

## Strategic competence without playbooks

Agents should become strategic primarily by perceiving decision-relevant signals, preserving them, learning contextual patterns, maintaining calibrated ActorModels, reasoning over probable responses, and valuing trajectories. The objective is not to upload hundreds of persuasion tricks or static branches.

## Extensibility law

A fourth sibling is admitted only when it owns a distinct semantic claim that cannot be faithfully composed from admissibility, belief, and utility, has stable provider-neutral contracts, independent failure semantics, and demonstrated recurring consumers.
