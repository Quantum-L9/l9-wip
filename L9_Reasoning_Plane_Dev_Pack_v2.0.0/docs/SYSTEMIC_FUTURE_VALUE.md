# Systemic Future Value

This document supersedes the v1.2 `ExpectedLeverageValue` concept.

## Definition

`SystemicFutureValue` is the expected counterfactual improvement in future reachable utility caused by an intervention, excluding value already counted as its immediate effect.

## Human shorthand

Humans may call this “leverage.” Machine contracts should not use an undefined leverage score.

## First-class dimensions

- capability unlock;
- recurring cost reduction;
- future work elimination;
- future decision improvement;
- reusable asset value;
- dependency unlock;
- future information improvement;
- action-space expansion;
- relationship/reputation effect;
- other declared effect.

These dimensions are explanatory labels. The value is derived from counterfactual future-state deltas, not by summing labels.

## Topology

Systemic future effects may propagate one-way, bilaterally, in closed loops, or through meshes. Cycles are unrolled through time, novelty/state deltas are valued once, and loops terminate under convergence controls.

## Evidence and calibration

Predicted future value carries model/evidence provenance and uncertainty. Realized downstream effects are recorded separately and may calibrate future estimates.
