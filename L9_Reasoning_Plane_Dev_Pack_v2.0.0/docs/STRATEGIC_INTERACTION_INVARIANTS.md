# Strategic Interaction Invariants

**STR-001** Other actors are endogenous parts of the decision environment when their behavior can respond to our choices.

**STR-002** Assertions made by strategic actors are observations, not automatically facts.

**STR-003** Strategically generated evidence must account for sender incentives when material.

**STR-004** A best response is conditional on an explicit opponent/other-actor strategy or belief model.

**STR-005** An equilibrium is a solution concept under declared models, not truth, policy, or execution authority.

**STR-006** A deterministic engine may calculate a mixed/randomized strategy; stochastic execution requires explicit authorization and reproducible randomness receipts when used.

**STR-007** Repeated-game consequences such as reputation, relationship capital, future bargaining position, and recurring transaction friction belong in future-state valuation when material.

**STR-008** Commitment may have positive strategic value even when it destroys optionality; both effects must be modeled without double counting.

**STR-009** Information acquisition and information disclosure are distinct decision problems.

**STR-010** Recursive beliefs and action/reaction lookahead are bounded by explicit depth, horizon, resource, and Value-of-Computation controls.

**STR-011** Strategic interaction is currently a composed triplet pattern, not a fourth reasoning sibling.

**STR-012** Game theory is a method family, not the node identity.

**STR-013** Predictions about another actor are distributions/hypotheses, not promises that the actor will behave as predicted.

**STR-014** Positive-sum, mixed-motive, zero-sum and coordination structures are materially distinct and must not be silently conflated.

**STR-015** Strategic influence does not require deception. Truthful signaling, sequencing, commitment, framing, and incentive design may create cooperative value.
